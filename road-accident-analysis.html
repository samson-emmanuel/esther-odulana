Road Accident Analysis
(2021 – 2022)

Evaluating road accident data with a focus on casualty impact, severity, and contributing conditions to surface actionable safety insights.

Problem Statement & Analytical Questions

The objective of this analysis is to evaluate road accident data with a focus on casualty impact, severity, and contributing conditions. The dashboard was designed to provide data-driven answers to the following key questions:

Primary KPIs

  • What is the total number of casualties resulting from road accidents?
  • How are casualties distributed by accident severity and vehicle type?
  • What percentage of total casualties does each severity level represent?

Secondary Insights

  • How do monthly casualty trends compare year-over-year?
  • Which road types and surface conditions are associated with the highest casualties?
  • What is the relationship between casualties, area type, and light conditions?

Analysis & Key Findings

trending_up Trend & Seasonality

From 2021 to 2022, data shows consistent casualty spikes in February, indicating a recurring seasonal pattern likely linked to winter road conditions.

directions_car Vehicle Type Impact

Casualty counts vary significantly across vehicle types, with specific models recording higher incident frequencies during peak hours.

wb_twilight Environmental Conditions

The relationship between casualties, area types (urban vs. rural), and light conditions (day vs. night) highlights critical risk factors for drivers.

road Infrastructure Factors

Road types and surface conditions correlate high casualty rates with specific road geometries and maintenance levels, pointing to infrastructure improvements.
