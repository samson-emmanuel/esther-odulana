Terry Gerry
Electronic Sales Analysis

Building a star schema data model from disconnected department datasets to unlock revenue insights and drive strategic sales decisions.

Company Overview

Terry Gerry is an electronics retail company operating multiple stores across different countries and states, selling products across categories including Computers, Audio, Cameras, and Home Appliances.

Problem Statement

Datasets from Product, Sales, and Store departments lacked explicit revenue information. Management was unable to translate operational data into clear business insights or evaluate sales performance effectively.

Technical Approach

ETL & Modeling

  • Imported CSV & PDF data from Procurement, CX, and Sales departments.
  • Extracted a unique Calendar table from Sales data for time intelligence.
  • Built One-to-Many Star Schema relationships using Power Pivot.
  • Defined Primary Keys in Product/Store tables and Foreign Keys in Sales table.

Analysis & Calculations

  • Used Pivot Tables for high-level aggregated data views.
  • DAX (Orders): Created using DISTINCTCOUNT for unique order numbers.
  • DAX (Revenue): Calculated as Quantity Sold × Unit Price.
  • Applied interactive filters and slicers for country-level drill-downs.
